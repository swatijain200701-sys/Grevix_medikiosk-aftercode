## 2. Feedback Collection & Verification Process

1. **Public Demonstration Access**: Testers visit the deployed MediKiosk application and test both:
   - The bilingual patient intake flow (`/kiosk`) with synthetic/fictional data.
   - The sample clinical triage review (`/dashboard?sample=true`).
2. **Accessible Form Links**: Direct links to the Google Form are placed prominently in:
   - Landing page navigation header
   - Dedicated landing page feedback strip
   - Patient intake completion screen
   - Sample staff review dashboard
3. **Receipt & Analysis**: Genuine responses from testers are reviewed for recurring friction points (e.g. font sizes, touch targets, clarity of medical terms in Hindi/English, or offline recovery cues).
4. **Iterative Improvement**: A concrete code improvement addressing specific tester critiques is implemented, committed, and documented below.
